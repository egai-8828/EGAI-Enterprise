# EGAI Enterprise

**An AI-powered business assistant for enterprise decision-making.**

EGAI Enterprise is a B2B AI platform under development, designed to help medium and large organizations analyze business information, understand financial performance, and make better-informed decisions.

## Vision

Our goal is to make enterprise AI more accessible through specialized business intelligence capabilities, bilingual interactions, and secure organization-aware workflows.

## Core Capabilities

- **Business Intelligence:** KPI analysis, business reporting, and performance insights.
- **Financial Analysis:** Financial data interpretation, reporting, and decision support.
- **Document Intelligence:** Analysis of business documents and structured data.
- **Bilingual Experience:** Arabic and English support, including mixed-language interactions.
- **Enterprise Architecture:** Designed around authentication, role-based access, and organization-level data separation.

## AI Technology

EGAI is designed to integrate with Anthropic Claude through Amazon Bedrock.

The planned AI architecture includes:
- Enterprise-focused AI responses.
- Specialized business workflows.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) for company documents.
- Secure backend-based model access.

The Claude integration and production deployment are currently under development and subject to provider access approval.
